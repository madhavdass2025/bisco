2. Electronic PIN (ePIN) Operations

A Earning Wallet to ePIN Generator Engine

Active members can convert their accrued commission earnings into reusable ePINs to register or upgrade new downline members:

  1. Go to the Earning Wallet to ePIN Generator section on the Dashboard.
  2. Select the package voucher type (e.g., Voucher 300).
  3. Click Generate ePIN Now.
  4. The package amount is deducted automatically from your wallet_balance, and a unique 16-character code (e.g., PN3A8F2K910L4M5N) is generated and sent via SMS.
B Redeeming an ePIN
  1. Select Electronic PIN (ePIN) under payment gateway options when purchasing a package.
  2. Enter the 16-character ePIN code and click Proceed & Activate.
  3. The subscription package activates immediately, and 12-level affiliate commissions are distributed up the sponsor chain.

3. Business Scheme & Compensation Management (Admin)

Super Admins can access the Schemes Manager (`admin/schemes.php`) directly from the dashboard navbar.

A Editing Existing Affiliate Commission Matrices
  1. Click on the 12-Level Affiliate Commission Matrix tab.
  2. Adjust the Classic Points, Basic Points, or Team Points allocated for any sponsor level (Level 1 through 12).
  3. Click Save Level to apply the changes immediately.
B Editing Existing Rank Requirements & Rewards
  1. Click on the 12-Tier Rank Schemes & Incentives tab.
  2. Edit the Rank Name, Required Downlines, One-Time Rank Incentive Bonus (₹), Monthly Incentive (₹), or Duration (Months).
  3. Click Save Changes.
C Creating New Business Rank Scheme Tiers
  1. Click the green Create New Business Rank Scheme button.
  2. Specify the new rank title, required team count, one-time cash bonus, and monthly payout details.
  3. Click Create Scheme Tier to add the new rank to the system.
